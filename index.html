DevOps Engineer @ Eurobank

I am a DevOps Engineer at Eurobank (formerly Hellenic Bank) in Nicosia, Cyprus. I hold an MSc in Cyber Security, Privacy and Trust from the University of Edinburgh and a BSc in Computer Science from the University of Cyprus.

I am responsible for keeping internal tooling up to date and patched with zero downtime, alongside the cloud-native infrastructure, CI/CD automation and observability that support large-scale banking systems. More recently, my work has extended to AI software factories and to helping engineering teams adopt AI securely through AI gateways.

My research background lies in blockchain data management for IoT workloads, with publications at IEEE MDM and BlockDM, and in anonymous communication, where my MSc thesis evaluated the performance and anonymity of mix-networks.
